## New

- **Small Yahu, a small window for links from other apps.** Links you click in Mail, Slack or Messages open in a small one-page window instead of a new tab in your main window. ⌘O moves the page into your main window as it is, without reloading; ⌘W or Esc closes it. ⌥⌘N opens an empty one, and Settings › General turns the link behaviour off.

- **Password manager extensions connect to their Mac apps.** The 1Password, Bitwarden, KeePassXC and Proton Pass extensions can now talk to their desktop apps, so they unlock with the app instead of asking you to sign in again. In 1Password, add Netnyahoo once under Settings › Browser › Add Browser.

## Fixed

- **Extension popups open from the Extensions menu again.** Extensions installed from the Chrome Web Store, such as 1Password and Bitwarden, did nothing when opened from the Extensions menu or a shortcut before being pinned. Their popups open now.

## Smaller

- ⌥⌘N now opens Small Yahu; Open Split Pane stays on ⌃⇧=.
