date: 2026-09-26
headline: Autocomplete stops putting words in your mouth.
---

## Fixed

- **Typing in the address bar is never lost or reordered.** Typing quickly while the address bar filled in a suggestion could drop a letter, put letters in the wrong order, or leave the suggestion in the middle of what you typed ("msail.google.comf"). The suggestion and what you type no longer race each other, in the New Tab page, the command bar and the sidebar address field.
- **Autocomplete fills in the site, not a whole link.** Typing "m" could fill in a long sign-in address from your history, shown from its end. It now completes to the site ("mail.google.com"), shown from the start, and Enter goes where the field says. Backspace removes the filled-in part first.
