## Fixed

- **Swipe back after switching profiles.** Trackpad swipes could keep targeting the profile you had just left and stop responding. They now follow the profile shown in the window, including when switching straight back. Sidebar swipes no longer disappear into the page.

- **A new swipe cancels the previous profile animation.** An animation waiting to start could resume after your next swipe began and move the sidebar against your fingers. It now stays cancelled.

## Smaller

- Update checks now go through netnyahoo.com. Settings explains the anonymous version and daily counts sent with them.
- Refreshed the bundled ad-blocking lists.
