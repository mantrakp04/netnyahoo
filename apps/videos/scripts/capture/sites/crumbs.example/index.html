Brown butter oat cookies

Makes 18 · 35 minutes · ★★★★★ (2,104)

Brown the butter until it smells like toffee, then let it cool before it meets the sugar. Rest the dough overnight if you can wait. You can't. Nobody can.

Ingredients

  • 200 g butter, browned
  • 180 g dark brown sugar
  • 1 egg + 1 yolk
  • 150 g rolled oats
  • 200 g plain flour
  • Flaky salt
