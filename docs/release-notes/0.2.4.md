## New

- **Sync, end to end encrypted, with no account.** Settings › Sync keeps bookmarks, history, pinned tabs and groups, settings, passwords and your other Macs' open tabs in step, per profile. It syncs through a folder you choose, iCloud Drive by default, and everything in it is sealed with a key from a 24-word recovery phrase that only your Macs know, like Dia's. Save the Recovery Kit when you turn it on; on another Mac, choose Connect with Recovery Phrase. The overflow menu lists your other devices' tabs. Turning sync off never removes anything from this Mac.

## Fixed

- **Leaving full screen takes the video with it.** Leaving window full screen with the green button or ⌃⌘F left the page's video full screen, with the sidebar and toolbar hidden until you pressed Esc.
- **Full screen stays put across profiles.** With two profiles in a full-screen window, a video going full screen in the other profile took the window out of full screen.
- **AppleScript can open windows at an address.** `make new window with properties {URL: …}` failed with an error; Raycast and other scripts that open pages in a new window work now.
