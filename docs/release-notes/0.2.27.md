## New

- **Share a page from Netnyahoo.** The site controls have Share…, which opens the Mac's share sheet, and File › Share and a tab's right-click menu list AirDrop, Messages, Notes and the rest. It shares the page's clean link and title.

## Fixed

- **Password managers reopen their popup after you unlock them.** Unlock 1Password or KeePassXC in its Mac app and its popup now comes back by itself, as in Chrome. That step failed before, which could leave the popup empty until you clicked the toolbar icon again.
- **Extension popups fit what they show.** They grow and shrink with their content, up to Chrome's 800 × 600, instead of keeping their largest size.
