## Fixed

- **Menu commands no longer crash the app.** In 0.1.0 and 0.1.1, every command in the menu bar crashed Netnyahoo, whether clicked or triggered by its shortcut (⌘T, ⌘W, ⌘N, ⇧⌘T, ⌘1–9…). The menu items pointed at the wrong method.
- **Passkey sign-in works.** Chrome's passkey dialogs (use a saved passkey, use your phone or tablet, security key, PIN) opened behind the window, so sites like Google waited forever at "Complete sign-in using your passkey". They now appear over the page. Passkeys saved in Netnyahoo use Touch ID, and passkeys on your phone work through the QR code. iCloud Keychain passkeys still wait on Apple's approval.
