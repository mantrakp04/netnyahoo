## New

- **Bring your tabs from Dia.** In the importer, choose "Dia: open and pinned tabs (via Dia)": Netnyahoo asks Dia for each profile's pinned and open tabs, and they arrive in the matching profiles, pinned tabs pinned and in Dia's order, open tabs in an "Imported" group. macOS asks once whether Netnyahoo may talk to Dia; it only reads, and changes nothing in Dia. Dia doesn't share its custom tab names, colours or folders, so those don't come across. Importing twice doesn't duplicate anything.

## Smaller

- Tabs imported from any browser keep their original order.
