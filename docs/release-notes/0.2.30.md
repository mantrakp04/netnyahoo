## Fixed

- **The toolbar hides while you scroll with tabs in the sidebar too.** "Hide toolbar while scrolling" only worked with tabs across the top: with tabs in the sidebar and the toolbar over the page, it stayed put however far you scrolled. Now it tucks into a thin strip with the site's name as you scroll down, and comes back when you scroll up, click the strip or switch tabs. It's in Settings › Tabs, on by default; with the address bar in the sidebar there's no toolbar to hide.
