## Fixed

- **Autofill suggestions can be picked again.** Chrome's dropdown under a form field (saved usernames, passkeys, "Use a passkey from another device", addresses, earlier entries) appeared but ignored clicks and Enter. Chrome only lets a suggestion be picked once the browser has gone idle, and our engine never told it so.
