## New

- **Pinned tabs as rows, like Arc.** In the Liquid Glass sidebar, favourites stay as tiles at the top and pinned tabs sit as rows under them, above the divider. Pin a tab, or drag it above today's tabs, to pin it as a row. Right-click to choose Add to Top Apps, Move to Top Apps or Move to Pinned Tabs. Tabs you've already pinned stay tiles.

## Fixed

- **The Liquid Glass sidebar looks like Arc's.**
  - The glass is a neutral, cool grey instead of taking on the profile's colour.
  - The window buttons move up to Arc's position, and everything under them follows.
  - Tab names are black (white in dark mode) with more room between rows.
  - The selected favourite is a raised, lighter tile.
  - The sidebar's edge no longer has a dark band next to the page.

  Dia's style is unchanged.
