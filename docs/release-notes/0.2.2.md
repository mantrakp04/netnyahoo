## New

- **The sidebar address bar works like Arc's.** With the address bar in the sidebar, clicking it (or ⌘L) opens a dropdown right from the field: the full address to edit, a site-info button, and your current and recent pages underneath. The sidebar header follows Arc's layout, and Downloads sit at the bottom of the sidebar.
- **Developer Tools do device mode.** The device toolbar (phone and tablet emulation, with device presets) works docked and undocked, ⌥⌘I and ⌥⌘J close docked Developer Tools like Chrome, ⌥⌘J opens on the Console, and View › Developer › Inspect Elements (⌥⌘C) starts the element picker.

## Fixed

- **Closing a pinned tab keeps it pinned, like Dia.** ⌘W, Close Tab or a middle-click closes the page behind a pinned tile and frees its memory, but the tile stays, back at its pinned site; click it to load it again. ⇧⌘T puts the page back into the tile. Tabs in pinned tab groups do the same, and new pinned tabs no longer sometimes unpin themselves.
- **Switching profiles is quick again.** A page dot or ⌃1–9 shows the other profile in about a tenth of a second (it took about half a second), and you can switch again straight away instead of waiting for the app to catch up. Swipes start moving with your fingers.
- **Developer Tools no longer go blank** after docking them back from their own window, and undocked device mode keeps the emulated page.
- **F12 toggles Developer Tools like Chrome,** and closes their own window. That window is titled "Developer Tools" with the page's address, like Dia, and opens where you last left it.

## Smaller

- Middle-click closes tabs in the sidebar.
- Escape cancels the ⌃Tab recent-tabs switcher without switching.
- Site icons in the sidebar address dropdown show in light mode too.
- The app is a little lighter: an unused styling library and other dead code are gone.
