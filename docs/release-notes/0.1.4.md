## Fixed

- **⌘-scroll no longer zooms with a trackpad.** Resting a thumb on ⌘ while scrolling with two fingers zoomed the page. It scrolls now; pinch to zoom instead. With a wheel mouse or a Magic Mouse, ⌘-scroll still zooms.
- **Chrome's own tab hover cards and zoom bubble no longer appear over the page.** Chrome's windows only come forward for its dialogs now, such as passkey prompts.
