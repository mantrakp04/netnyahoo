## New

- **Share a page from Arcadia.** The site controls have Share…, which opens the Mac's share sheet, and File › Share and a tab's right-click menu list AirDrop, Messages, Notes and the rest. It shares the page's clean link and title.

## Fixed

- **Clicking outside the address bar closes it.** A click on the empty sidebar, another sidebar row, the tab strip or Little Arcadia's bar left it open with its suggestions; only the page closed it. Now any click outside does, as Esc does, and the click still does its own thing.
- **Password managers reopen their popup after you unlock them.** Unlock 1Password or KeePassXC in its Mac app and its popup now comes back by itself, as in Chrome. That step failed before, which could leave the popup empty until you clicked the toolbar icon again.
- **Extension popups fit what they show.** They grow and shrink with their content, up to Chrome's 800 × 600, instead of keeping their largest size.
