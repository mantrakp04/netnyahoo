## Fixed

- **Pages that stop responding are reported again.** In 0.2.20, the new pictures of dragged tabs left a debugger attached to every tab you had looked at, and Chrome doesn't flag a hung page that has one, so "Page isn't responding" never appeared. Tabs no longer keep one.

## Smaller

- A tab you drag shows its icon and title instead of a picture of its page, for now.
