## Fixed

- **Ad blocking works when the app runs from the disk image or Downloads.** 0.1.0 loaded uBlock Origin Lite from inside the app, so Chrome wrote its filter indexes into the app bundle. That broke the code signature on first launch, and from a read-only location (the DMG, or the temporary copy macOS runs a quarantined app from) nothing was blocked. uBlock now runs from a copy in the data folder. Existing profiles carry over.

## New

- **Where's Big Yahu?** replaces Chrome's offline dinosaur: when you're offline, find Big Yahu in a crowd of donors, lobbyists and very generous friends. Also at `netnyahoo://yahu`.
- **Translucent window**, like Dia's: the desktop blurs through a tint in dark and light mode, and the window goes solid when it's inactive.
- **Profile paging**, like Dia's: swipe and the next profile's sidebar slides in while the window colour cross-fades. Page dots in the sidebar footer switch profiles too. Works with a trackpad, a Magic Mouse, or a wheel mouse's horizontal scroll or Shift-scroll, and ignores the "Swipe between pages" setting, as Dia does.
- **Pinned tiles take the favicon's colour** when selected, as in Dia.
- **Import from Helium and Dia** (bookmarks, history, open tabs, passwords, cookies), and **from Safari directly** (bookmarks, Reading List, history, open tabs) with Full Disk Access. Chrome and Brave now ask for Full Disk Access instead of silently missing from the list.

## Smaller

- The New Tab page's intro plays once per tab, not every time you switch back to it.
- The profile name in the sidebar header no longer gets cut off ("Hom").
