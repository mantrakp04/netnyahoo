immunity
noun · im·mu·ni·ty

1 : the quality or state of being immune; especially : a condition of being able to resist a particular disease

2 : exemption from a duty or liability that is granted by law to a person or class of persons

"He claimed immunity, as he did on most Tuesdays."

3 : freedom from something unpleasant, such as pop-ups
